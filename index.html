Where AI Meets Local Expertise — Est. 2026

Planning still runs on three broken systems

None of the tools travelers actually use today were built to do the whole job — plan it, trust it, and get through it safely. We confirmed this with 77 travelers before building anything — see next.

01 — GENERIC AI & MAPS

Route-based, not you-based

ChatGPT and Google Maps can list ten cafés near you. Neither knows which one the owner will wave you into for a free coffee, or which street to avoid after dark.

02 — SOCIAL & BLOGS

Sponsored, generic, and stale

A creator's one good day in a city two years ago isn't a plan for your Tuesday afternoon — and you can't tell sponsored content from a genuine recommendation.

03 — BOOKING MARKETPLACES

Trust flows one way, then stops

You review the guide after the fact. Nobody verifies who you are, and once the tour ends, there's no safety net left if the rest of your trip goes wrong.

Why India-first

Why the gap stays open

70%+ of experience operators worldwide are small, unverified operators — too small and scattered for big OTAs to bother with. That's exactly the layer our planned KYC + trust score is designed to organize.

The journey, in three stops

54.1% of travelers want to plan it themselves. 52.7% still want expert help "when it depends." So Virun is built AI-first, human-optional — never AI-only or agency-only.

STEP 1

Plan it yourself

Pick a city, language and budget, then interests — adventure and heritage first, where 94.6% of surveyed guide demand sits.

STEP 2 — PLANNED

AI creates your trip plan

A full itinerary you stay in control of and can edit — not a locked-in agency package.

STEP 3 — PLANNED

Top up with a local, in-trip help

Planned local toggle if the city is unfamiliar (only 28.4% want a guide outright). Planned live translation, scam alerts and SOS.

How Virun makes money — and earns it

Three revenue lines, priced against what surveyed travelers actually said they'd pay — plus a step-by-step plan for the first 6 months that anyone on the team can pick up and run.

1 · Marketplace commission — the core engine

15–20% take rate

On guide bookings priced at what travelers said they'd pay: ₹200–600 for a 4–6 day online-guide tier, ₹1,000–3,000 (₹2,000–2,500 typical) for an in-person guide. This is the only revenue line planned for the first 6 months after launch.

2 · Virun+ subscription — add after traction

Unlimited AI planning

Unlimited itineraries, translation, and priority guide matching for frequent travelers — all planned, switched on once we have repeat users. Switched on once we have repeat users worth subscribing, not on day one.

3 · Partnerships & B2B — add once supply is proven

Hotels, boards, corporates

Affiliate commission from hotel & restaurant partners, tourism board deals, and corporate travel packages. Only pursued after Phase 1 supply is in place — partners want proof, not a pitch.

Go-to-market — build it real first, prove it in one city, then scale

Months 0–2: finish product, Razorpay escrow, KYC pipeline. Months 2–6: Hyderabad, 50–100 guides, real paid bookings, then Vizag. Months 6–18: first 10,000 bookers. Months 18–36: 15–25 cities, ~5,000 guides.

Where the moat actually is

The AI planning layer will commoditize fast — every competitor will have one within a year. The defensible part will be the planned verified-local trust graph and safety infrastructure (KYC, trust scores, escrow, SOS) that compounds with every booking and is expensive for a pure chatbot or content platform to copy.

Known risks, and the concrete fix for each

  • Guide supply is the hard constraint, not traveler demand: only 24.3% of surveyed travelers would become guides — fixed by running a dedicated recruitment funnel alongside the app from day one, not after launch.
  • Cold start: fixed by proving the model fully in Hyderabad — real guides, real bookings, real payments — before opening Vizag, instead of spreading thin across many cities from day one.
  • Guide quality / fraud: planned fix — mandatory government KYC, a visible trust score, escrow on every payment, and community moderation.
  • Big OTAs adding AI planning: our edge is the verified human + safety layer underneath the AI, which a pure software competitor can't copy quickly.
